A New Twist on Arcade Classics

Inspired by classic space invaders, Bounce Invaders turns traditional arcade shooting on its head.

Your spaceship moves automatically from side to side, bouncing back when it reaches the edge of the screen. Time your taps to launch upwards while your weapons blast through enemy formations.

Clear the aliens, take down flying UFOs and make your way to the next battlefield above.

How to Play

Your spaceship automatically moves left and right, changing direction when it reaches the edge.

Tap the screen to bounce upwards. Your weapons fire automatically, so focus on timing your movements, avoiding enemies and lining up your shots.

Destroy alien invaders, collect rewards and advance upwards through the levels.

Upgrade your ship, complete challenges and aim for the highest score!
