R.G.B

Aim of the Game? Get the highest score you can!

Tap either left or right on the screen to change the blocks around, catch the right coloured circle in the same coloured square and score a point.

Watch out... It gets trickier as you progress. Do you have what it takes to get in the Hall of Fame?

R.G.B is a game of pure frustration... Think you can become the master?

Instructions

Tap left or right to move the blocks and match the colours.
